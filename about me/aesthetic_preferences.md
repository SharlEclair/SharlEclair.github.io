# Visual Identity: "The Computational Atelier"
* **Vibe:** A late-night 2 AM workstation / research archive. Feels like a scientific laboratory where experiments have been left running in the dark.
* **Color Palette:**
  * **Warm Paper:** `#E9E1D2` (Editorial background)
  * **Deep Basalt Ink:** `#151419` (High-contrast text & hardware)
  * **Signature Violet:** `#7055D9` (Primary telemetry accent)
  * **Muted Oxblood:** `#B6424D` (Secondary warning/error accent)
  * **Muted Gold:** `#C6A96B` (Hardware details/PCB traces)
* **Materials & Textures:** Refractive frosted crown glass, bead-blasted matte titanium, glowing analog phosphors (oscilloscopes/CRTs), subtle scanlines, and razor-sharp laser hairlines.

# Typography
* **Dual-Register System:** A strict pairing of expressive, high-fashion **Humanistic Serif** (for display/editorial elegance) with cold, tabular **Monospace** (for metadata, code, and strict engineering discipline).

# Media & Entertainment
* **Cinema & Storytelling:** Thriller films, true crime documentaries, procedural investigations, and high-tension neo-noir.
* **Music & Audio (Wide Polarity):**
  * *Hyperpop / Adrenaline:* Charli XCX (*Brat*).
  * *Hip-Hop / Production:* Kanye West (*Graduation*), Kendrick Lamar, Drake.
  * *Psychedelic / Analog Synth:* Tame Impala (*Currents* — specifically Robert Beatty's album art aesthetic).
  * *Cinematic / 80s Synthwave Noir:* The Weeknd.

# Design Likes
* High-end scientific instrumentation (microscopes, calipers, wind-tunnel sting mounts).
* Optical phenomena (refraction, chromatic dispersion, caustics, laser beams).
* Editorial Swiss graphic design (grids, crop marks, forensic registration symbols).
* Clean data visualization without heavy charting libraries (e.g., 2D flow lines, vector grids).
